## 1. Context

A real estate business in Dubai whose sales team answered buyer questions by searching brochures, documents and FAQs by hand.

## 2. Problem

Every buyer question meant a manual search through brochures and FAQs. Answers were slow, inconsistent between salespeople, and the search itself took time away from selling.

## 3. Decisions

- **Retrieval over the documents the team already trusted**, not a model answering from general knowledge.
- **Every answer shows its source.** A salesperson or a buyer can check the brochure page behind a claim.
- **The assistant qualifies, people close.** It asks qualifying questions and captures the lead; a person takes over the conversation.
- **Lead capture goes straight into the CRM**, so nothing lives only in a chat log.

<Placeholder>Owner to supply: options rejected (for example a general chatbot without retrieval, or one that could quote prices or availability without a source).</Placeholder>

## 4. Architecture

Brochures, FAQs and documents are indexed for retrieval. A buyer enquiry is matched against that index and answered in natural language, with the source shown beside the answer. The assistant asks qualifying questions, writes the lead into the CRM and hands qualified leads to the sales team.

## 5. Where humans stay in the loop

<ReviewMark label="Sales team takes over" />

The assistant never commits the business to anything. It answers from documents, shows where the answer came from, and passes qualified buyers to a person. The handoff is the point of the design.

## 6. What runs in production

- Retrieves relevant answers from company documents, property brochures and FAQs.
- Responds in natural language and shows the source behind each answer.
- Handles customer enquiries and asks qualifying questions.
- Captures lead details in the CRM and hands qualified leads to the sales team.
