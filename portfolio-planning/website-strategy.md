# Website Strategy

**Status:** Prepared for Gate 2
**Depends on:** Approved brand strategy (Gate 1)

## Primary purpose
Convert a business decision-maker's interest into a booked call with Jagdish, by demonstrating that he puts AI into the systems a business already runs, safely, and stays accountable from discovery to production.

## Target audience (approved order)
1. Owners and decision-makers at operational SMEs (trade, real estate, finance, logistics, mobility, education).
2. Partner agencies needing a technical execution partner.
3. Companies seeking fractional or full CTO leadership.

Design and copy are written for audience 1. Audiences 2 and 3 are served by the About page and the leadership content, not by separate pages.

## The one message
> AI that works inside your business, built and owned end to end by one accountable technical lead.

## Desired visitor journey
1. **Arrive (Home, first screen).** Within five seconds: name, what he does, for whom, and the booking link. The portrait and the serif establish "a person, senior."
2. **Believe (Home, middle).** Three or four short proofs: the human-in-the-loop principle, the production ERP, the team scale, the method. Each is a fact from the CV.
3. **Inspect (Work).** The visitor opens one or two case studies that match their industry. Each opens with the business problem, then the decisions. The diagram language makes the architecture legible to a non-engineer.
4. **Trust (About).** How he works, the seven steps, how he leads teams, the stack. The partner-agency and CTO audiences find themselves here.
5. **Act (Contact).** Book a call. Expectations stated: length, who takes it, what to bring. Secondary: email, LinkedIn.

The journey is two or three clicks long. Nothing on the site exists that does not shorten it or strengthen it.

## Conversion actions
| Priority | Action | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Book a call | Navigation (persistent), Home opener, end of every case study, Contact page | Scheduling provider to be confirmed by owner. Stated as "30 minutes, directly with me" or similar once confirmed. |
| Secondary | Email | Contact page, footer | Address to be confirmed. |
| Secondary | LinkedIn | Contact page, footer | From CV. |
| Tertiary | Download CV (PDF) | About page | The uploaded PDF, once the owner confirms it is final. |
| Not recommended | Contact form | — | Adds a backend or third-party dependency for no gain over booking plus email. Revisit only if the owner wants enquiry capture without a call. |

## Evidence required for credibility
- The four selected projects as case studies, with architecture decisions and the human-review step made explicit.
- The eight-industry table.
- The scale facts: 25–50 engineers led, 8 industries, 5 LLM families, 7-step method.
- The seven-step method itself.
- The named stack.
- A real portrait.
- **Not available and not to be fabricated:** client logos, testimonials, quantified outcomes, awards. If any become available with permission, they slot into the case studies without changing the structure.

## Content hierarchy
1. Positioning statement and booking link.
2. Human-in-the-loop principle and production proof.
3. Case studies.
4. Method and leadership.
5. Stack and industries.
6. Education and contact details.

## Recommended scope
Eight pages: Home, Work (index), four case studies, About, Contact. One shared navigation and footer.

**Deliberately excluded**
- Blog or notes: no existing writing; an empty or thin blog damages credibility. The architecture leaves room to add a Writing section later without redesign.
- Testimonials: none available; the brief forbids inventing them.
- Services or pricing page: engagements are scoped per client; pricing on the site would undercut the discovery-first method. Services are expressed through the method on About.
- Contact form: see above.
- Animations beyond hover, focus, one page-load transition, and one-time diagram draws.

## Success criteria
- A first-time visitor can state what Jagdish does and for whom after the first screen.
- The booking link is reachable from every page without scrolling.
- Each case study can be understood by a non-technical reader in under three minutes.
- The site is recognisably his, not a template: a reviewer should be able to describe its visual identity in one sentence.
- Lighthouse performance and accessibility scores of 95 or above on mobile, measured, not assumed.
